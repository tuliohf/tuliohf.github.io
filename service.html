Chair, since 2025

RILEM TC ASM

Atomistic Simulations for Cement-Based Materials: Recommendations and link to Experiments

The committee brings together the molecular simulation and experimental communities working on cement-based materials. It is producing recommendations on force fields, atomic models and simulation protocols, and state-of-the-art reviews in working groups on topics such as chemical transformations.

Opening letter, RILEM Technical Letters 11 (2026) · RILEM
