RILEM TC ASM

Atomistic Simulations for Cement-Based Materials: Recommendations and link to Experiments

TC ASM brings together the molecular simulation and experimental communities working on cement-based materials. It reviews the atomic structures and force fields used to model clinker phases, cement–water suspensions and hydrated pastes, recommends modelling tools, and benchmarks simulation results against experiments.

Chair
Tulio Honorio
Deputy chair
Aslam Kunhi Mohamed

Working groups

WG1Atomic structures and force fields
Curated structures for C-(A)-S-H, ASR products, AF-phases and other phases; review of classical and machine-learning force fields
WG2Chemical transformations
Dissolution, precipitation and growth; advanced sampling and free-energy methods
WG3Interfacial processes and confined fluids
Adsorption, ion and water transport, sorption-induced deformation
WG4Mechanical, thermal and electromagnetic properties
Elastic, thermal, conductivity and dielectric properties compared with experiments
WG5Mesoscale modelling
Effective interactions from the molecular scale and coarse-grained simulations

Planned outputs: a topical collection in Materials and Structures, an open-access database of structures and tools, and tutorials on recommended modelling practice.
